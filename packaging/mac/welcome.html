Spectra @VERSION@

Spectra is a spectral freeze effect: it captures a moment of sound and stretches it into a pad.

This installs Spectra as a VST3 (FL Studio, Ableton Live, Reaper, Bitwig, Studio One) and as an Audio Unit (Logic Pro, GarageBand) into /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins.

After installing: open FL Studio, go to Options → Manage plugins, click Find plugins, and Spectra appears under effects. In Logic Pro or GarageBand, find it under Audio Units → Shreyas → Spectra.
